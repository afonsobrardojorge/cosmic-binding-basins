# v0.2.0 — Revised Wording, Expanded Figures And TEC-3 Geometric Extension

This release prepares a cleaner follow-up to the archived Zenodo v0.1.0 record.

## Main changes

- Replaced the rhetorical "dark energy does not destroy..." wording with a more technical formulation:
  "Dark energy is treated here as a large-scale boundary-setting term for gravitational membership, rather than as an internal dynamical disruption mechanism within already bound systems."
- Converted figure labels and explanatory text to English.
- Corrected the validation-plot boundary label to:
  `T_Lambda = 1 boundary`.
- Expanded the manuscript figure set:
  - TEC phase diagram.
  - Median log10(T_Lambda) by synthetic class.
  - Pilot literature validation with error bars.
  - Synthetic R ~ M^(1/3) scaling test.
  - Dynamical dark-energy extension.
- Retained TEC-3 as a proposed geometric extension based on Weyl/tidal curvature dominance, not as a validated result.

## Recommended Zenodo description

Version v0.2.0 revises the manuscript wording, converts all figure labels to English, corrects the validation-boundary legend, expands the figure set, and keeps TEC-3 as a proposed geometric extension. The work remains an exploratory preprint and pilot validation, not a claim of a new physical law.
